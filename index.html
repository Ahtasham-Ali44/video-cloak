4 — TikTok / Instagram ke liye (native video upload)

Sach ye hai: TikTok ya Instagram par video upload karke, video par click se destination khulwana mumkin nahi — dono apps me video par tap sirf play/pause karta hai, aur caption/comment ka link bhi clickable nahi hota. Ye un apps ka apna rule hai, koi bhi tool use nahi badal sakta. Lekin wahi result in 3 tareeqon se milta hai:

Tareeqa 1 — Bio link (sab se asan, free): apni video normal Reel / TikTok ki tarah upload karo. VideoCloak wala link apne profile ke bio / website wale box me lagao. Video ke akhir me aur caption me likho: "Link bio me hai". Viewer bio par click karega → seedha destination.

Tareeqa 2 — Instagram Story link sticker (video par hi clickable): wahi video Story me lagao, Link sticker add karke us me VideoCloak wala link dalo. Story dekhne wala sticker par click karega → destination. Ye wahid free tareeqa hai jis me video wali screen par hi clickable link aata hai.

Tareeqa 3 — Paid Ad ka CTA button: Instagram / TikTok par video ko Ad ki tarah chalao to video ke neeche button (Learn More / Shop Now) aata hai, usi button me destination link lagta hai. Click → destination. Ye paisay wala tareeqa hai.

Templates upar Step 3 me bane hue link aur title se khud bhar jati hain — pehle Step 1–3 pura karo.

Kaise kaam karta hai?

1. Tum video ka .mp4 link aur apna destination link dete ho.
2. VideoCloak ek link banata hai: /go?to=…&vid=…&img=…
3. Jab koi social app is link ka preview banata hai, usay video + poster + title milta hai.
4. Jab koi insaan click karta hai, wo seedha tumhare destination par pounch jata hai.
